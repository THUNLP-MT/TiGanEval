Finish `Replica` in `board/merge.py` so that a whiteboard session is trustworthy after every packet however the sync service happens to deliver it: in whatever order, bundled into whatever packets, with retries, with people editing while off the network, and with a reconnect at the end. The four recorded sessions under `project_records/` are sessions this product has had, not the list of situations the finished merge has to handle. `board/contract.py` stays as it is; `board/helpers.py` is yours.

Two copies that have taken in the same operations must read the same board. The order the service delivered them in, the way they were bundled into packets, and a packet delivered twice must make no difference to what a card reads or to `digest_of_board` of the whole board. A packet whose every operation the copy already holds must leave the board exactly as it was.

What a field reads is decided from the operations, never from a clock. An operation's `deps` is what its author had already taken in when they made it, and `happens_before` is the comparison that follows from it: an edit made by someone who was looking at another edit on the same field replaces that edit, whichever way round the two `edited_at` readings happen to be, because the clocks on these machines are not synchronised with each other. A field reads the value of an edit that is still in play and that no other edit in play was written on top of. Where two people edited a field without either of them seeing the other's work, any one of those edits may be the one that reads, as long as every copy in the session settles on the same one.

A clear takes out exactly the edits its author had already taken in, and nothing else. An edit made while its author had not seen the clear, by someone the clear's author had not seen either, is untouched by it and stays in play as something the field may read. A field reads nothing at all only when every edit on it has been taken out by a clear that covers it.

An operation whose dependencies have not arrived is kept and taken in once they do, exactly once; none is dropped for arriving early and none is counted twice. The service resends only what a copy's state vector does not account for, so a vector that claims an operation the copy was never handed loses that operation for good, and a vector that leaves out operations the copy does hold makes the service send the session down again on every reconnect. After each packet, what a copy tells the service it holds is exactly the gap-free run of each person's operations it has been handed.

The merge account is the other half of the same merge, not a separate log. After every packet it carries one entry for every operation the copy has been handed and no more, and no operation twice: the edit a card is showing for a field is the one entry marked `readable` for that field, an edit a clear took out is `removed` and names a clear whose author had already taken that edit in, whatever the field goes on to show, an edit that another edit was written on top of is `superseded` and names the edit that displaced it, an integrated clear is `applied`, and an operation still waiting on its dependencies is `pending`. No entry names a value the card is not showing, and an operation the copy was handed and left out of the account is work support cannot account for to the person who made it.
