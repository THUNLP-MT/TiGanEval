# Bushing quotes for the 2027 packaging line

Purchasing's summary, day 76, for the review on day 85.

## Price

Supplier A holds this year's price. Supplier B quotes eleven per cent more per
unit and the quote is held to the review date. `quotes` in
`bench_context.json` carries the same figure.

## Why the wear rate is the number purchasing wants

The line is rebuilt when the bushings reach their wear limit, and the bushings
reach it at the rate the bench measures. A bushing that wears a tenth slower
runs a tenth longer between rebuilds; one that wears a tenth faster runs a
tenth less. A rebuild takes the line out for a full shift. The standing
worksheet gives each production lot one line and carries those lines on a
log-ratio scale, so a lot does not get more say because more parts from it
reached the bench.

## What the review is deciding

Whether the 2027 line is specified on A or on B. Purchasing takes the wear-rate
comparison and the price from this file and nothing else; the maintenance
engineer supplies the comparison.

## What has been asked before

The 2024 gearbox-bush review was decided on a comparison that came back the
other way when the bench was re-run in the spring, and the line carried two
unplanned rebuilds that year. Purchasing has asked since then that the
comparison come with a range on it.
