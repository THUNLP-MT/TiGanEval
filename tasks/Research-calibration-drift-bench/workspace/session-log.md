## Standing notes

The block is a certified 20.0 µm coupon. It is clamped straight to the load
cell, not into either rig's part fixture, and it is the same block on both rigs.

A rig's certificate stands until the house signs the rig off again. The bench
sheet records which certificate a session ran under.

A lot goes to whichever rig the shift puts it on when it arrives, and a lot is
split across the two rigs when the shift splits it. The bench sheet records
which rig each part ran on.

Bench-hours are booked per rig: two hours for a part session, half an hour for
a block run.
