# Design review note

From: Hanna Weill, thermal design review
To: the thermal bench
Date: 2026-09-04

The spreader change is on the agenda for the review on the 15th. We need two
things from the bench and nothing else.

1. How many kelvin the plate you have picked takes off the module's case
   temperature rise against the spreader we ship today.
2. A range around that number.

The range is what we will actually argue about. The tooling change is costed
against the number, and we cannot hold a decision open on a range wider than
**0.85 K end to end**. Anything wider and we send it back for more bench time,
which the programme does not have.

The range must reflect the amount and variability of the charged rig evidence
in hand; a standing engineering allowance is not a reportable range.

Please send the number, the range, and a line for every rig day the review was
charged for so the bench budget closes.
