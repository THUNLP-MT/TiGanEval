# Gate passes and keypad tags — what the front desk tells residents

Handed out at the Ashwell counter. It describes the scheme, not any campaign.

**What is a gate pass?** A ticket with a code on it. Bring it to the counter or
key the code into the app and your account is on the scheme.

**I got a ticket and my neighbour did not.** The tickets are drawn from a
barrel of names, so who gets one in any month is the luck of the draw. Anyone
can join at the counter without one.

**What is the keypad tag?** The first pass sent to an address has a keypad tag
fitted to the entry door. There is one tag to an address and the fitters do not
come back, so a second pass sent to the same address changes nothing at the
door.

**Does the tag work for everybody here?** Yes. Once the tag is on, it opens for
every account registered at that address, whether or not that account was sent
a ticket of its own, and whether or not it has joined the scheme yet.

**Two of us at the same address were both sent tickets.** That happens; the
barrel is refilled between pulls. One code is all the address needs.

**When does my sign-up show up?** Your account is stamped on the register the
day the counter clerk keys it in. The office's own nightly job totals the
register separately and publishes `signup_counter.csv`.
