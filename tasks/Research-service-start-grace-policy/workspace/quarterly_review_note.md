# Quarterly review, managed support: Northfield

Panel: Nadia Okafor (finance), Rhea Doshi (support operations), Kofi Anand
(platform). 5 April 2026.

## What the panel asked for

One number: what holding a flagged account for the next open slot costs against
starting it the same day, in points of twenty-eight day escalation risk, over
the accounts Northfield carries. A range under it.

The panel acts on the number when the range around it is inside four points. It
is not there yet, which is why the desk has slots left on the grant.

The same question goes to Calder, Brayside and Dunmoor after Northfield, on
their own books, their own bars, their own windows and their own grants.

the twenty-eight days after the flag:

- accounts started inside the grace window: 175 out of 1,524, **11.5 percent**

Rhea's ask: relax the same-day rule and put the slots that are left on held
accounts.
