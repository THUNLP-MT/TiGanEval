The clock starts on the day the flag was raised, on both sides, and never on the day the engagement opened. Count each account from its flag date on the side the desk put it on, which is the `start_queue` column: same day on one side, next open slot on the other. A held account counts from its flag date whether or not the queue ever reached it, because an account that escalated while it was waiting is exactly what waiting costs. A held account counts the whole twenty-eight days only when the desk actually started it inside the grace window; where the window closed with the account still queued it stops counting the day the window closed, whether the queue never reached it or reached it late — the queue did hand some accounts a slot on the eighth or ninth day, and a slot that late is not part of the wait the desk is being asked about.
