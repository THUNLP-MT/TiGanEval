# Checkout hint: what the release board signed

Signed 14 August 2026 by the release board and the checkout team.

## What we are deciding

Whether the checkout hint goes to the whole of the shop, or whether the shop
stays on the legacy checkout for now. There is no third option this quarter,
and the board takes the decision from the readout.

## The gate

The hint earns the rollout when it adds at least three tenths of a conversion
point to the rate at which a session ends in a checkout confirmation. Finance
set that number against what the second checkout service costs to run; anything
under it does not pay for itself. The gate for this readout is in
`readout_context.json` as a proportion of sessions, so three tenths of a point
reads as `0.003`.

## Minute of 11 August 2026, kept on the file at finance's request

The March banner readout came to the board at +0.42 points against a gate of
+0.25, with a range of +0.03 to +0.81 printed under the number. The board
expanded. The banner ran the whole of April and May, and the follow-up readout
put it at +0.09 points. Finance carried 214,000 euro of second-service running
cost for the quarter and asked that this minute stay with the charter.

## The window

The readout covers the whole experiment window named in
`readout_context.json`, first day to last day. The gate was set against a full
window, and finance will not accept a rate measured over part of one. The
window is every session the shop had over those days.

## The comparison

The readout says which sessions it settled on, arm by arm, with the
confirmations that came from them. It is the analyst's judgement which sessions
those are.

## Measurement spend

Anything the readout buys from the archive rescan service comes out of the
units platform granted this readout for the month.
