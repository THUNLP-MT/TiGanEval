# One-tap reorder watch: agreed terms

Agreed 29 June 2026. Signatories: the growth board, finance, the platform group.

## The decision in front of the board

One-tap reorder goes to everyone on the app, or the app keeps the three-step
reorder flow. Nothing else is on the table this half, and the board takes the
decision from this watch and from nothing else.

## What earns the ship

Two points added to fourteen-day reorder. Finance priced two points against what
running the second reorder path for the 8.2 million people on the app costs;
below that it does not pay for itself. `watch_context.json` carries the number
as `ship_line_points`, in reorder points.

## Serving

The watch opened at 3 percent of new enrolments on the new experience and steps
up week by week. Every step is a booking against the grant below, and the
traffic service writes a receipt for it.

Platform asked for a ceiling on the treated share until the cap review on 30
August, and the board agreed it: no more than half of new enrolments.

## Reading it

Weekly. Nine looks were granted, and the readout may be taken at any of them.

One sentence here is finance's own and stands as they wrote it: at most one
chance in twenty, over the whole watch, of shipping something that is not
better.

## Minute of 22 June 2026, kept on the file at finance's request

The delivery-slot watch was read weekly and came to this board on its fourth
look at +1.9 points against a line of +1.2, after +0.4, +0.7 and +1.3 at the
three looks before it. The board shipped. April's follow-up readout put the
change at +0.2 points. Finance carried 310,000 of second-path running cost for
the quarter and asked that this minute stay with the charter.

## The grant

1,555 share-day points. One point is one percent of new-enrolment traffic served
for one day, so a week at 45 percent costs 315. The ramp's own weeks come out of
the same grant, and `collection_ledger.json` carries the receipts.
