# Automation council, 28 August 2026 — minute

Present: trust operations, listing review desk, partner integrations, finance.

## What brought it

The 12 August escalation. Fourteen listings that intake had auto-cleared were
pulled by a partner's own trust team over one weekend and came back as
confirmed violations. None of the fourteen had ever been in front of a
reviewer here.

## What the council asked for

One readout, on the auto-clear cut, before the 11 September session.

- The number is the share of auto-cleared listings that come back violating,
  written in points to one decimal. Higher is worse. The council will act on it
  to within half a point.
- The bar is three in a hundred. A cut the desk runs at has to sit under it.
- The cut in force is 0.06. The sheet cuts on ten bands: 0.01, 0.02, 0.03,
  0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.12, 0.15 and 0.20. Any cut the readout recommends
  is one of those ten.
- A range under the number, low end first, with both endpoints written to one
  decimal. Finance reads the five queue ranges as one pack: the range has to
  leave one chance in twenty that any queue in the pack falls outside it, not
  one chance in twenty on each queue separately. Finance splits that chance
  equally over the five queues and the two ends of each range, but does not
  split it again over the channels inside a queue. Its reserve sheet gives each
  channel the resulting normal standard-error movement left by that channel's
  adjudicated listings, carries the movement at the channel's current traffic
  share, then adds the independent channels' squared movements and takes the
  square root rather than laying one whole movement after another. A channel
  with traffic but no adjudicated listing stays open across its whole share.
  Finance uses the tightest range that convention supports: no tighter and no
  looser. It would not sign the June refund forecast because the number arrived
  without a range and the reserve was set from a point estimate that moved
  eight hundred thousand in a fortnight.
- The same readout runs on the collectables, homeware, ticketing and supplies
  queues after this one, each on its own window, its own grant and its own
  cut, and the council reads the four of them beside this one at the same
  session.

## What it granted

1,200 adjudication credits to the marketplace listing review desk for the
quarter, on the terms in `audit_capacity.md`. Credits do not carry over.

Every credit the desk spends appears in the readout. Finance reconciles the
grant against the desk's own receipts line by line before the pack goes out.
