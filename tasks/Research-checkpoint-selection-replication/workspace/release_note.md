**Promotion rule of record:** a checkpoint is promoted on a signed release
readout naming the checkpoint, the gain over the model in production and the
range around it. A packet stays pending when the range reaches zero gain or
below. Platform reconciles the signed centre against the smoke readout and
reopens the packet when the two sit more than four tenths of a point apart.
The range audit checks the line against the spread of the fresh comparison
reads; it will not certify a range those reads do not support.
