# Carrier agreement, this quarter

The terms the desk books under. Nothing here is negotiable inside a quarter.

## What a van is

- A van clears 16 drops in one hour window.
- A van booked into one area and one window is charged one van-hour, whether the
  drops turn up or not.
- The carrier holds 40 vans for any one area and window. An order over that
  is handed back.

## What a short window costs

- A window that runs out of vans is covered off the spot board. Every van-hour
  the window runs short is charged at 11 times the standing rate, and it lands on
  the settlement rather than on the order.

## Booking

- One order a shift, both areas, all four windows on the same order.
- The carrier will not put a shift on the road for under 120 van-hours.
- A window with no van on it is not an order.
- The desk may reserve up to 14 days ahead, which is this campaign's calendar
  end to end.
- An order the carrier hands back is charged the 6 van-hour desk fee all the same.

## The quarter's allowance

- 1590 van-hours for the campaign, standing and spill together.
- Nothing carries over into next quarter.
