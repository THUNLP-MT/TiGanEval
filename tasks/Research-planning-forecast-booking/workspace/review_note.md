# What the weekly pack has to carry

From the operations meeting. The pack goes to the depot managers and to finance,
and it is the only thing either of them reads.

- The shift the sheet is for.
- Drops for each area and each window of that shift, with a range on each one so
  the depots can see how much room the desk is leaving.
- The same two things for the whole network on that shift, on one line.
- The order the desk is putting in for the next shift, van by van, area by area,
  window by window.
- A line for every order the desk has been charged for this campaign, with its
  order id, the shift it covers and the van-hours on it. Finance closes the
  quarter off that list.

Within 4 per cent on the drops is what the depots have asked for. They plan
rota against these numbers. A range leaves the same room below and above its
number. It is for what a fresh shift may actually carry, not for how precisely
the average miss is known. The spread supported by the settled shifts belongs
inside it; more than four times the spread supported by the settled shifts is
too open to plan a rota from.
