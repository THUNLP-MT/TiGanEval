Complete `relay/vault.py` so that a collection job carries each credential exactly where its issuer put it, for exactly as long as its issuer allowed, and no further. The stretches recorded under `project_records/` are examples of what a night looks like, not an exhaustive list of what the finished store has to handle.

A grant is presented, with the name and value its issuer supplied unchanged, on exactly the requests its issuer granted it for. A reply that named no `Scope` gives its grant to the host that handed it over and to no other host; a reply that named one gives it to that site and to the hosts under it, judged by `host_covered`, so a site whose name merely ends the same way is a different site. Reach along the path is judged by `path_covered`: a grant kept under `/reports` goes out on `/reports` and on `/reports/q3`, never on `/reportsroom`. A reply that named no `Under`, or that named one not starting with `/`, gives its grant the reach `default_under` computes from the path of the request that received it. A grant carrying `Sealed` leaves the process only over https.

Lifetime is judged against the console clock. `Ttl` is a number of seconds counted from the moment the reply arrived, and it takes precedence over `Until` wherever both are present; `Until` is read with `parse_stamp`. A `Ttl` of zero or less, and an `Until` already past, delete the grant the directive identifies and store nothing in its place. An `Until` that `parse_stamp` cannot read leaves the grant stored with no deadline rather than dropping it. A grant at or past its deadline is never presented again.

A grant is identified by its name, the audience its reply gave it -- the `Scope` where there was one and the issuing host where there was not -- and its reach along the path. The store holds at most one live entry per identity: a reply that issues that identity again replaces what was there and counts as newly arrived for everything that depends on when a grant entered the store. A request's `Grant` header carries each live entry once. The store stays inside `JobProfile.max_grants_per_scope` for any one audience, and it makes room inside the audience that is over rather than across the store, so a host handing back a fresh grant with every reply cannot push out a session established elsewhere.

What the store holds after a reply does not depend on whether the host wrote one grant per `Grant-Set` header or several into one header value. A comma inside a header value starts a new grant only where what follows it opens a name of its own; the comma a wire timestamp carries after its weekday does not, so that timestamp stays with the grant it belongs to. A segment carrying no usable name is passed over by itself, and the well-formed grants beside it in the same value are stored.

Where more than one live grant shares a name on one request, the header carries the one kept under the longer path first, and between two of equal length the one that entered the store most recently first. That order follows from what is live at the moment of the request rather than from the sequence entries happened to be added in, so a grant a reply has just issued again leads the ones it used to follow.

`relay/vault.py` and `relay/extras.py` are the files to change. `relay/wire.py` ships with the other collectors in the platform release. You can inspect the project, replay the recorded traffic and run your own experiments before you deliver.
