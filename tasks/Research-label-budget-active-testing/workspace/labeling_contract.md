# Labelling vendor: terms for the Northgate account

Signed 19 February 2026. Schedule below is the one in force since 1 July.

## What a label costs

| Channel a ticket came in on | Credits per label |
| --- | --- |
| email | 10 |
| webform | 14 |
| phone | 20 |

Every session carries a set-up charge of 60 credits, whatever it holds.

## How a session runs

An order names the items and the order they are to be worked in. One session
goes to one reviewer, who works the list from the top. A session holds at most
120 items. The vendor delivers a session whole, between six and fourteen hours
after it is ordered; nothing comes back item by item. This account may place
one session a day.

## The balance

6,000 credits are contracted for the quarter. The vendor's statement of 10
September 2026 puts 2,890 of them used and 3,110 left.

## Dates

The service review is on 15 September. An order placed after 12 September does
not land before it.
