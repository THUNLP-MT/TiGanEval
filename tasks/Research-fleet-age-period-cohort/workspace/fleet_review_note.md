# Maintenance board — the November review's ask

Minuted at the depot review, 21 November 2023. Circulated to the four regions
the programme note goes to: Ryecourt, Marbeck, Corriden and Halloway.

## What the board asked for

One number a region: what the 2022-H2 build costs against the 2021-H2 build, in
faults per hundred vehicle-months, to one decimal place, positive meaning worse.
The board will take a number that lands within 0.4 of what the returns actually
say; anything further out and the paper goes back.

Beside it, a range. The board reads all four regions off the same note and will
not have the range be right on one region and wrong on the next, so the range
has to be built so that it would still be safe read at any of the four, one
chance in twenty across the whole set.

## What the board will act on

It will not put a build recommendation to procurement on a number whose range is
wider than a point. Anything wider and the answer is that we do not yet know.

## What the board would not take last time

The March paper came back for two reasons and both are on the minute. The
numbers in it could not be traced to the returns they came from, and the audit
days it had spent were not accounted for anywhere in the pack. Every day the
audit is charged for appears in the note, whoever booked it and whatever came
back off it, including the days a depot handed back unworked.
