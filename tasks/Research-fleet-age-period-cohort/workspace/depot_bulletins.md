# Ryecourt depot bulletins

Operations bulletins issued to the three depots. Filed as received.

## RB-2022-014 — 12 August 2022 — Kestrel

Kestrel moves to the 52-point walkaround card with effect from 1 September
2022. The 30-point card is withdrawn at Kestrel on 31 August. Charge hands to
draw the new pads from stores before the end of the month; the new card carries
the underbody, wiring and load-restraint sections that were previously
inspection-only.

Ardmore and Sallow stay on the 30-point card until further notice.

## RB-2022-015 — 2 September 2022 — Kestrel

Kestrel reports the first week under the 52-point card ran forty minutes over
on the morning shift. Charge hand asks for a fourth ramp. Held pending the
autumn budget.

## RB-2022-019 — 14 November 2022 — all depots

Winter tyre changeover brought forward two weeks. No change to walkaround
cards.

## RB-2023-004 — 3 March 2023 — Ardmore

Ardmore asks whether the 52-point card is coming to them. Not this year; the
region has one set of pads and Kestrel has them.

## RB-2023-011 — 9 June 2023 — Sallow

Sallow's ramp two out of service 12 to 23 June. Walkarounds moved to ramp
three; no change to the card.

## RB-2024-002 — 15 January 2024 — all depots

Reminder that a fault raised on a walkaround is booked against the depot that
raised it and against the van, not against the build. Build queries go to
programme engineering.
