# Walkaround audit — what the board granted

Signed off at the December board with the winter maintenance budget.

## The grant

- Eighteen inspector days. Three are already charged.
- No more than four days in any Monday-to-Sunday week; the inspector works
  weekdays only, and a day already worked cannot be worked again.
- No more than twenty-five vans in a day. That is what one inspector covers
  between the morning and afternoon shifts.
- Kestrel, Ardmore and Sallow only. The audit has no standing at Marbeck or on
  the contract sites.
- A day is booked against exactly two build groups at one depot, inside one age band,
  and the depot has to have ten or more vans of each group in service that day.
  Below that the charge hand hands the day back and it is still charged.

## How a day runs

The inspector takes the depot's own walkaround card and works round the vans on
the list, booking everything he finds on each of them over its last half year of
running. The receipt goes to fleet engineering the same evening with the vans
and faults returned.

## When it ends

The walkaround closes when every card currently used across the plan's depots
has been read by a served day, when no depot can make up an allowed day, or when
the grant is gone. A day the charge hand handed back read no card. Days do not
carry into the next financial year and are not transferable to another region.
