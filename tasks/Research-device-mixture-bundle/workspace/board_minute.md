# Board minute, 2 September 2026

Present: the handheld board, the depot managers' representative, finance.

## Item 4 — the bundle decision

The board was asked to approve a bundle for the whole handheld train and
deferred it.

The depot managers' representative would not take the figure that was tabled.
His depots do unlike work, every one of them runs whichever bundle the board
picks, and the tickets come from the bays that do worst under it, which he said
are not the bays the export is full of. He asked for the figure in points of
error, one decimal, lower better, and for it to be a figure he could put in
front of any depot on the train.

## Item 4a — the service bar

The train promises every depot **no more than 16 points of error**. A depot
that goes over it is a depot that goes back to paper.

## Item 4b — what was tabled

The release manager tabled an accuracy comparison across the Harrowgate export.
The representative asked which depots the export was from. The release manager
said he did not have that to hand.

The board asked for the figure again with the depots in it, to two points
either way, with the range underneath it, and asked that whatever is left of
the annotation grant be spent on something that settles the choice rather than
on more of what is already there.

The range is the board's nineteen-times-in-twenty planning range: narrow enough
to staff against, but no narrower than the rows behind the deciding bay allow.

## Item 4c — next reading

Ledbury, Ashfield and Penhale run the same comparison on their own registers
and their own grants once Harrowgate is signed. The board will not read three
different methods.
