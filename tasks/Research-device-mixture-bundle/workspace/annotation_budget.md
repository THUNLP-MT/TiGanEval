# Annotation grant, Harrowgate Q3

Raised by Dario Feng, release manager, handheld train. Approved by the platform
group on 19 June 2026.

## The grant

**1,200 credits.** They do not roll over and the quarter closes with the board
meeting. Every request the desk touches is billed against them, and
`capture_receipts.json` is the desk's own record of what it charged.

## What a request may ask for

One duty profile, one firmware and one capture condition, and between **20 and
120 rows**, inclusive, in a single request. The desk will not split or combine
a request itself.

## Prices

| Cell | Credits a row |
| --- | --- |
| counter | 1 |
| backroom | 3 |
| yard | 3 |
| night | 3 |
| the reference card, on either firmware | 1 |

A counter annotator sits in the same building as the desk. Everywhere else the
desk sends somebody out, and the price is three times the counter rate.

**A request the desk turns away still costs 20 credits.** It logs the reason on
the receipt and charges the minimum for the trip.

## Requests handed back so far

Two of the four requests on file came back with nothing.

- `hg-damaged` asked for 60 damaged captures from counter units on 4.2. The
  desk logged "no units in that cell" and billed 20.
- `hg-oversize` asked for 150 backroom captures on 4.1. The desk logged "over
  the rows a request may ask for" and billed 20.

The other two were counter requests and came back full. That leaves 920 credits
in hand.
