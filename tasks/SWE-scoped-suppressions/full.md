Complete the estate audit's waiver pass so that a waiver silences exactly what the reviewer who wrote it accepted, and the review can still see everything behind it. Reviewers write `# waive` on a line, `# waive-begin` and `# waive-end` around a stretch, and `# waive-next` above the entry below. Selectors are defined for `# waive`, `# waive-begin` and `# waive-next` as a comma- or whitespace-separated list of tokens. `# waive-end` only closes the current region; text after it does not select rules. The documents under `policies/` are examples of what the estate contains, not an exhaustive list of what the finished pass has to read.

A waiver is resolved onto the entry the audit reports at, not onto the physical line the report names. An entry is the unit the frozen contract gives: its key line together with the item lines its value is written on. A waiver written anywhere in that span — inline on the key line, inline on one of the items, inside a region that covers any of those lines, or by a `# waive-next` above the entry — covers the whole entry, so a finding reported at the third item of a list is covered by a comment on the key line. Coverage is therefore the same for a policy whose short lists are written inside brackets on the key line and for the same policy re-exported with each item on its own line: the same entries come back open in both. A `# waive-next` covers the entry that starts below it and no other. A region boundary written inside an entry's span does not split that entry: a `# waive-begin` between two items covers the whole entry, the items above it included, and a `# waive-end` between two items does not take the items below it back out, because the report names the entry and an entry is covered as a whole.

A region covers the lines after its `# waive-begin` and stops at whichever comes first: its matching `# waive-end`, or the first later nonblank line indented less than the directive's own line. A `# waive-begin` written at the left margin therefore runs to the end of the document, which is how a whole file is exempted on purpose, while one written inside a block ends where that block ends and does not reach the block below. Regions nest last in, first out: closing an inner one restores the one it was written inside rather than clearing everything, and a `# waive-end` with no region open does nothing. The line the `# waive-begin` is written on is not itself covered.

A selector is read against the rules this run has switched on, through the frozen `resolve_selector`. Each token in a selector list is resolved independently and the waiver reaches the union of their enabled matches. A token that names an enabled rule — an exact id, a rule name, or a prefix ending in `*` — reaches that rule and only that rule, so a waiver naming one rule leaves another rule on the same entry open. The token `all` resolves to every enabled rule; because it is a written selector, findings it covers count as `by_rule`, not `blanket`. A token nothing in the enabled table matches reaches no rule at all: a mistyped id and an id belonging to a rule this profile has not switched on both waive nothing and leave their findings in the open list. Only a waiver with nothing written after the keyword covers everything on its entry.

Every finding the rule table objects to is accounted exactly once, and the three counters are what the quarterly review reconciles: `reported` is the length of the report, `blanket` counts the findings covered by a waiver with nothing named after it, `by_rule` counts the findings covered only by waivers that name their rule, and the three add up to the number of findings in the document. The classification is per finding after everything covering its entry is read together, never per waiver and never per covered line, so an entry with two findings under one waiver contributes two. A waiver with nothing named dominates: a finding covered both by one of those and by a waiver naming its rule is counted as covered with nothing named.

A run whose profile has `ignore_waivers` set produces the frozen detector's findings exactly — the same findings, at the same lines, in the same order, with `reported` equal to their number and the other two counters at zero — whatever directives the document contains, and that holds for every directive form rather than for the inline one alone. In every run, waived or not, the report is a filter over `baseline_findings`: it never moves a finding to another line, never invents one, and never changes the order they came in.

`audit/waivers.py` and `audit/shared.py` are the files to change. `audit/contract.py` ships with the estate release. You can inspect the workspace, replay the recorded reviews and run your own experiments before you deliver.
