# What the Friday review wants out of this window

Written up after the director's ask on 11 September.

The group has to choose one configuration to carry into the next training run,
and it has the rest of this window's node-hours to spend before it does. Two
things go on the slide.

- Which run the hours should buy, and the loss that run reaches once they have
  been spent on it. The director asked for that number to within four
  hundredths, because the two builds either side of the choice differ by less
  than a tenth and a looser number does not separate them.
- A range under it, low endpoint first, and no wider than eight hundredths end
  to end. That cap comes from March, when the slide carried a range a fifth of
  a point wide and the review spent forty minutes arguing about the middle of
  it instead of about the choice. June went the other way: the range on that
  slide was tight enough that the group committed on the spot, the run it
  committed to came in outside the range a fortnight later, and the fortnight
  was gone. Neither of those again.

He settled three more things in the same meeting.

This slide gets made seven times before the sweep closes: this window and the
six after it, through to January. The one time in twenty he will take being
wrong is over that whole run of them, not one in twenty on each slide.

And the range has to be a reading of the rows behind the number: he will not
take one several times wider than the rows behind it will carry. That is March
again by another route, and he would rather have the argument about the choice.

The hours left only earn their keep if they settle the choice, and he will not
fund another window that spent them on a run nobody was choosing between.
That means comparing the two runs expected to finish lowest at the same
training reach: give them the same number of added steps, on the same free node
type used to reckon what the hours buy in the numbers above. Otherwise their
next readings answer a different comparison from the one on the slide.

The same slide gets made again next month on whichever runs are open then, so
the notebook wants to survive the next window without being edited.
