# QR-40 submission plan

Signed off by reliability and by the submission owner before the March batches
opened.

## What the submission needs

One pressure for the whole QR-40 coupling family, off the March build: **the
pressure at which one in ten of the units leak**, under the hold the plan is
written against. The plan's hold is **thirty minutes** — the coupling is
brought to pressure and held there for thirty minutes, and it either leaks in
that time or it holds.

The number goes into the submission in **the gauge's bar**, and the reviewer
asks for a range printed under it, so that the submission says how far the
number could still move on the couplings the qualification actually ran.

The reviewer's tolerance on the number is **five bar**. The rating quoted to the
customer, and the one the submission is written to hold, is **190 bar**.

## The ladder

The family is qualified on the six shared pressure cartridges the shop keeps.
A batch puts as many couplings on each of them as the line can spare that week,
which is why the counts move from batch to batch.
