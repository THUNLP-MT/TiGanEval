# Pricing council, Tuesday 26 May 2026

Present: the pricing council, finance, growth operations.

## Item 4: the standing-score offer

The council wants one number from the Northgate window: **the observed
sixty-day ordering difference between the accounts the line offered and the
ones it passed over**, in points of order rate, with a range under it. This is
the campaign comparison at the line, not a claim about what the same account
would have done had its offer been reversed. Finance prices the offer against
the accounts the line turns one way or the other, and not against the book as
a whole.

For this readout, "at the line" means the accounts the weekly run scored
within five points of 61.0 on either side, using the score that run actually
read. A manager credit that by itself moved an account from below the line to
the offered side is manager selection rather than a decision made by the line,
so that account is outside this comparison. A credit that did not change the
side remains in it.

## The range

Finance's sentence, as they wrote it: *at most one time in twenty, over the run
of markets this goes to the council on, of putting a number in front of us that
the market will not bear out.* The slide carries **seven markets** and Northgate
is one of them. The same notebook produces all seven, so the range has to be
one that would have been safe at any of them.

## When the council stops paying for files

The council will keep buying review files while the range is worth narrowing.
Once the range is **five points end to end or tighter**, it stops: the decision
does not turn on anything finer than that, and the files are worth more on the
next market.
